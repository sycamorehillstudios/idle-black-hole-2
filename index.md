# Privacy Policy for Idle Black Hole 2: Devour

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

Sycamore Hill Studios ("we") makes Idle Black Hole 2: Devour ("the game"). This page explains what data the game handles and why.

## No accounts, no servers of our own

The game has no sign-in, and we run no server for it. Your progress is saved on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The game receives a record that a purchase was made so it can deliver it; we never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your disk is full or a new event starts). They are created on the device; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

## What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we don't use any analytics service of our own. We don't sell personal data.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
